# Course materials

Keep original files in their existing formats. Use descriptive filenames with lecture numbers or dates where useful.

```text
resources/
├── IN3050/
│   ├── notater/      Personal notes
│   ├── slides/       Lecture PDFs
│   ├── programmer/   Code, assignment briefs, and feedback
│   └── eksamen/      Exam information, papers, and solutions
├── IN3230/
│   ├── notater/
│   ├── slides/
│   ├── programmer/
│   └── eksamen/
└── lenker.txt        GitHub and course links by course and semester
```

Copy `lenker.example.txt` to `lenker.txt` and add your links. Download restricted UiO material manually. Keep credentials and dependency directories such as `node_modules` and `.venv` outside the course materials.

Git ignores the source materials to keep them local. Review the materials before adapting them into questions, explanations, and diagrams.
